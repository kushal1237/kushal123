ESP32 · GAS SENSOR · AUTOMATION

Smart Kitchen — Gas Leak Detector & Auto Fan

A prototype designed to respond when a gas leak is detected.

The gas sensor feeds readings to the controller. When a leak is detected, the system is designed to activate an exhaust fan and alert the user. The prototype helped me explore sensors, wiring, control logic, and how hardware can respond to a real-world problem.

ESP32Gas sensorRelay moduleFan automationIoT

This is a learning prototype, not a certified safety device. Do not rely on it as a replacement for approved gas detectors.
